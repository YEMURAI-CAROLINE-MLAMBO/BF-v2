# BendingForce v2: Conceptual Overview

## Introduction
The most natural way humans see the world is in three dimensions. However, for the last 50 years, our computers have forced us to look at flat, two-dimensional screens. While this is useful for reading text, it is "unnatural" for viewing 3D objects like hearts, engines, or architectural models.

BendingForce v2 changes this by moving the image from the screen into the space around you.

## The "Air as a Canvas" Concept
Traditionally, to see an image, you need a surface—a piece of paper, a movie screen, or a glass display. BendingForce v2 removes the need for a physical surface.

Think of it like this:
*   **The Atmospheric Canvas:** We use the air above the device as an uninterrupted canvas.
*   **The Conductor Analogy:** Just as lightning uses the air to conduct electricity, our technology uses the air to "conduct" and display light in 3D.

We aren't just projecting onto the air; we are using the volume of space above the device to reconstruct objects so they appear solid and real to the naked eye.

## Step-Out-of-Screen (SOOS)
When you use a BendingForce v2 tablet, the digital world is no longer trapped behind the glass.
*   **Depth:** Objects appear to float between 2cm and 30cm above the tablet.
*   **Look-Around:** You can move your head to see different sides of the object, just like a real physical item on your desk.
*   **Interaction:** You can reach into the air and "touch" the holographic object to rotate it, zoom in, or pull it apart.

## Why This Matters
By using the air as our canvas, we achieve several breakthroughs:
1.  **No Wearables:** You don't need heavy, uncomfortable glasses or headsets.
2.  **Collaborative:** Multiple people can stand around the device and see the same 3D object at the same time.
3.  **Natural Sight:** Your eyes focus on the object in space naturally, reducing the eye strain often associated with traditional screens or VR.

## Summary
BendingForce v2 is not just a better screen; it's a new way of interacting with digital information. By treating the air as a canvas and light as a conductor, we bring the digital world into our physical reality.
