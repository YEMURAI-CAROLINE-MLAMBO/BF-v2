# Gaming and Entertainment Use Cases

## Overview
The BendingForce v2 platform provides a unique canvas for immersive entertainment. By bringing 3D content out of the screen, it enables new genres of gaming and media consumption that were previously only possible with head-mounted displays.

## 1. Tabletop Spatial Gaming
Traditional strategy and board games are reimagined in 3D space.
*   **Living Dioramas:** Strategy games (RTS/Turn-based) appear as miniature 3D worlds floating above the tablet. Players can move their hands through the units to command them.
*   **Spatial Card Games:** Digital collectible cards that project high-fidelity 3D monsters or heroes when placed on the "field."
*   **Dungeon Crawler/RPG:** Players look down into 3D environments, seeing around corners by physically moving their head/eyes (utilizing the Micro-Prism Lattice parallax).

## 2. Spatial Cinema and Media
*   **Multi-View Sports:** Watch live sports events where the field is reconstructed in 3D on the tablet. Users can rotate the stadium to see the action from any angle.
*   **Interactive 3D Documentaries:** Educational media where viewers can "pull" objects (e.g., a dinosaur or a space probe) out of the video to examine them in 3D space.
*   **Music Visualizers:** High-energy spatial light patterns that react to audio, synchronized with the built-in haptic feedback.

## 3. Social and Creative Interaction
*   **Spatial Avatars:** 3D telepresence where callers appear as life-sized "busts" floating above the screen during video calls.
*   **3D Sculpting and Art:** Artists use a stylus or hand gestures to "carve" 3D objects in the spatial volume, allowing for intuitive 3D modeling without the abstraction of 2D screen projections.

## 4. Gaming Ecosystem Integration
To support the gaming community, BendingForce v2 will provide:
*   **Unity/Unreal Engine Plugins:** One-click spatial rendering support for the world's leading game engines.
*   **Spatial UI Kits:** Pre-built 3D menu systems and interaction patterns for developers.
*   **Low-Latency API:** Direct access to the HSU (Holographic Synthesis Unit) for frame-critical gaming applications.
