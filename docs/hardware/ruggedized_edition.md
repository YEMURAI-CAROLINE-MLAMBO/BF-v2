# Ruggedized Field Edition: BendingForce v2

## Overview
The **Ruggedized Field Edition (RFE)** is a specialized variant of BendingForce v2 designed for high-stakes, extreme-environment operations. It leverages the "Crystal Lattice" spatial imagery engine within a mission-critical chassis built to withstand the harshest physical conditions.

## 1. Environmental Survivability

### 1.1 Ingress & Shock Protection
*   **IP69K Rating:** Dust-tight and resistant to high-pressure, high-temperature water jets.
*   **MIL-STD-810H Certified:**
    *   **Drop:** Survives 2-meter drops onto concrete.
    *   **Shock/Vibration:** Validated for mounting in armored vehicles, rotorcraft, and high-speed maritime platforms.
    *   **Corrosion:** Salt-fog resistant for coastal and naval operations.

### 1.2 Thermal Management: Piezoelectric Active Cooling
To maintain the high-power requirements of the "Crystal Lattice" Spatial Light Engine in extreme heat (+60°C):
*   **Solid-State Fans:** Uses ultra-thin piezoelectric air-movers (no rotating parts) to maintain high-velocity airflow through a sealed graphene heat exchanger.
*   **Zero-Infiltration Design:** Air is moved through external-facing cooling channels, ensuring no dust or moisture enters the electronics or the SLE optical stack.

## 2. Field-Optimized Optical Stack

### 2.1 Sunlight-Readable "Crystal Lattice"
The RFE increases the output of the Micro-LED array to overcome direct solar interference.
*   **Peak Spatial Brightness:** 7,500 nits.
*   **Dynamic Contrast:** 1,000,000:1 (via local dimming on the Micro-LED array).
*   **Non-Reflective "Crystal-Glass":** A specialized diamond-like carbon (DLC) coating that reduces surface reflection to <0.1%.
